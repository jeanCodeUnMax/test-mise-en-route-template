# State Of Art Report Template

Use this file as the expected quality bar before turning a brainstorm into a PRD.

## ACTIVE_SUBJECT
What current project subject does this idea strengthen?

## QUERY
Search query pack for scientific, legal/regulatory, market, and implementation sources.

## SOURCES
- Paper/product/law/tool URL, with one-line relevance.

## RESEARCH_QUESTIONS
- What exact question must be answered?
- What result would change our decision?

## METHOD_VARIANTS
- Baseline method.
- Proposed method.
- Competing or adjacent methods.
- Cheap control or ablation.

## BASELINES_TO_BEAT
- Current local implementation.
- Open-source implementation.
- Paper benchmark.
- Commercial/product alternative when relevant.

## EXPERIMENT_AXES
- Model/data/hardware scale.
- Prompt or task family.
- Attack/failure class.
- Cost, memory, latency, quality, safety, compliance dimension.

## METRICS_AND_KPIS
- Quality metric.
- Cost/latency/memory metric.
- Safety/proof/compliance metric.
- Reproducibility metric.

## BENCHMARKS_AND_DATASETS
- Public benchmarks.
- Local benchmarks.
- Synthetic controls clearly marked SYNTHETIC.
- Real-world evidence required before claims.

## SCALING_OR_CROSSOVER_TESTS
Define the curve that would make the idea serious:
- quality vs budget;
- latency vs quality;
- memory vs quality;
- safety leakage vs compression;
- crossover point where the proposed method beats the baseline.

## EXTERNAL_BASELINES
Named papers, models, libraries, regulations, or products we must compare against.

## ALREADY_DONE
What the world already solved.

## USEFUL_INDICES
Methods, warnings, formulas, datasets, metrics, or plots to reuse.

## GAP_OR_DIFFERENCE_REQUIRED
What significant difference we must bring.

## MARKET_OR_REGULATORY_SIGNAL
Why anyone would care now: cost, law, risk, compliance, buyer pain, timing.

## LIMITATIONS_AND_KILL_CRITERIA
What would kill the idea or force it to backlog.

## TRANSFER_TO_PROJECT
What must be copied into the PRD, task graph, benchmark plan, or research note.

## GO_NO_GO
GO, NO_GO, MODIFY, or INCONCLUSIVE.

## WHY
Short decision rationale.

## NEXT_ACTION
PRD, NEW_HYPOTHESIS, BACKLOG, or REJECTED.
